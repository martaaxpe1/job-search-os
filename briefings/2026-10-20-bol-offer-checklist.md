# bol offer checklist: what to check, and the break-even with Rabobank

Use this when the bol offer arrives (final round with Ronald van Rijn: Thu 22/10/26). Ask Thijmen for anything the offer doesn't state, and ask for the terms in writing.

36h/week, fixed-term to 15/11/2027, scale 09.

Not in the totals: €1,400/yr development budget, about €83/month in home-working and internet allowances, and commuting costs.

## Where bol becomes better

All bol figures assume 8% holiday allowance on top of base, no 13th month, and 29 days of leave. The bonus is excluded: it's upside on top of everything below.

**1. Cash only (no pension, no bonus).** bol needs **€8,057/month**. That's below bol's posted minimum (€8,473), so any offer in the range beats Rabobank on yearly cash.

**2. Cash plus pension, the real comparison.** This depends on bol's employer pension contribution (as a % of the pension base):

| bol employer pension | bol needs at least (monthly base) |
|---|---|
| 20% or more | €8,009 |
| 15% | €8,295 |
| 10% | €8,607 |
| 0% (worst case) | €9,325 |

At your **€9,000** ask, bol beats Rabobank on total yearly pay as long as its employer pension is **4.3% or more**. At **€9,500** it beats Rabobank even with no pension at all.

**3. Per hour worked, if bol is 40h.** bol is about 165 more working hours a year (40h vs 36h, after leave). Rabobank works out to €71.73 per hour worked, including pension.

| bol employer pension | bol needs at least (monthly base) |
|---|---|
| 20% | €8,762 |
| 15% | €9,081 |
| 10% | €9,429 |
| 0% | €10,229 |

At **€9,000**, bol matches Rabobank per hour only if its employer pension is **16% or more**. At **€9,500**, it needs **9% or more**.

**Bonus (separate).** At 25% max of annual base, the bonus is worth €25,419 (at €8,473), €27,000 (at €9,000) or €28,500 (at €9,500). Count only what it has actually paid out, not the maximum.

## What to check in the offer

### Money
1. **Monthly base, and the full band for the level.** The posted range €8,473–9,533 looks like 80–90% of a band that tops out near €10,590 (my inference). Ask what the band is and where the offer sits in it.
2. **Contract hours.** 40 or 36? This drives the per-hour comparison above.
3. **Holiday allowance** on top of base, or included? **13th month**, yes or no?
4. **Employer pension contribution, as a % of the pension base.** bol says it covers "75% of the premium", which says nothing until you know the premium. Also ask for the offset and the provider.
5. **Bonus.** Target vs maximum, what it's based on (sustainability goals, company or personal results), whether it's prorated in year one, and what it actually paid out in the last 2–3 years.
6. **Other budgets.** Training or development budget, home-working allowance, mobility or lease options.

### Contract and security
7. **Permanent or fixed-term?** Probation length? This matters more than the leave percentages if family plans come up within 1–2 years. In bol's 2022 cuts, about 100 fixed-term contracts were not renewed.
8. **CAO or bol's own employee handbook?** Ask for the document itself.
9. **Side activities.** Is freelancing outside bol's industry allowed, and does it need approval?
10. **Notice period.**

### Family
11. **Maternity pay.** Does bol top up above the UWV cap? Rabobank pays 100% of salary for all 16 weeks. Without a top-up you'd get about 80%.
12. **Paid parental leave.** Does bol top up the 9 paid weeks? Rabobank pays weeks 1–4 at 100%.
13. **Any family terms beyond the legal minimum** (parental leave age limit, care leave at 100%).

Asking for the full terms of employment is a routine request. It doesn't reveal anything personal.

### Timing
14. **bol's decision date versus Rabobank's start (16/11).** Rabobank's outside-activities approval and contract signing are still open.

## Quick decision rule

- **Offer ≥ €9,000, 40h, employer pension ≥ 16%, permanent contract:** bol is better on every money measure.
- **Offer between €8,473 and €9,000:** bol pays more per year but probably less per hour. It comes down to pension, contract type and the bonus track record.
- **Fixed-term contract at bol:** contract security is then roughly equal to Rabobank's (fixed term with a written intention to convert), so decide on money, the role and the family terms.
