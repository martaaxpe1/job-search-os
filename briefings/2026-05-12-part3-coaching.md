## Interview Weakness Coaching

However, two documented weakness patterns from interview-history.md are directly relevant to the active pipeline and worth surfacing now:

### Weakness A: Domain passion signalling (surfaced: Adidas final round)
**Pattern:** Specialist panels at identity-driven companies evaluate whether you *see the world the way they do* — passion must be pre-loaded into the opening, not earned through process. Adidas feedback: "lost on 'inspire the team enough.'"

**Relevance to active pipeline:**
- **oneUp (Food):** This is a food-sector innovation consultancy. The Adidas pattern will repeat if the case presentation opens with methodology before establishing a genuine point of view on food innovation. Before presenting: draft a 2-sentence opening POV on food waste innovation that sounds like Marta's own conviction, not a framework. Draw from the Culligan/Pelican sustainability arc — reducing plastic, Give Power NGO, Delft water-at-festival research. The food-waste brief is adjacent to water-waste work she's done.
- **Philips Shaving (Director):** Less identity-driven than Adidas, more engineering/consumer-health culture. Domain passion required here is physical-digital product experience, not shaving as a lifestyle category. The Pelican story (hardware + IoT + consumer UX + manufacturing in China) maps directly. Lead with product-building specificity, not innovation philosophy.

**Drill:** Before the oneUp case presentation, practice a 90-second opening that starts with a POV on the food-waste opportunity space — what you believe is true that others miss — and lands on why oneUp's model is the right wedge. Run it with a timer. If it sounds like a consultant's hypothesis framing rather than a practitioner's conviction, rewrite it.

### Weakness B: Overqualification framing for IC roles (noted: career-plan.md)
**Relevance to active pipeline:**
- **Aquent/IKEA — Service Designer:** If this reaches an interview, the hiring manager will see 12 years of experience including 3 years leading a 15-person team with a €450K budget and reporting to global leadership. The risk: they assume Marta will be bored or leave quickly for a leadership role.
- **Unilever UFS — Experience Design Lead (surfaced May 11):** Same issue — the role is senior IC, and Marta's CV leads with leadership scope.

**Send-ready framing (practice until it sounds natural, not scripted):**
> "I'm deliberately choosing to go deep on craft here. The last 3 years were heavy on steering — owning the budget, running the stage-gate, managing upward. What I'm excited about in this role is being close to the work again. Having run a team, I know what good looks like from both sides, and I find I do my best strategic thinking when I'm embedded in the problem, not two layers above it."

Pair this with a specific IC deliverable from the experience library to make it concrete: the YouTube diary studies + metaphor elicitation, the Tikkie experiment design (10 experiments, not delegated — Marta ran the research), or the Pelican touchscreen UX/UI she designed herself.
