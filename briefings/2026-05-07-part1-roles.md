## Process Note (carrying forward)
The May 6 freshness rule helped, but today exposed a second failure mode the rule didn't cover: **third-party-aggregator staleness**. A Werkzoeken.nl listing for "Quooker Lead Commercial Product Manager" surfaced as a credible candidate; the role does not exist on Quooker's official portal. Aggregators often keep job posts visible long after the company has closed them. Three lessons updated for tomorrow:

1. **Company-native portal is the only freshness signal that counts.** Aggregator dates (Werkzoeken, Indeed, Glassdoor, even LinkedIn) can lag the company portal by weeks or months. From now on, for any role to enter Top Roles or Needs Manual Verification, the listing must be confirmed present on the company's own careers site — not just on an aggregator. If the company portal is on the network-blocked list, search-engine site:companycareers.com results are the next-best proxy and must be used.
2. **Honour the discarded-roles register.** Today's MSM re-surfacing was a process failure — the discard from May 6 should have been checked before tailoring. The "Discarded Roles — Do Not Re-Surface" section above is now part of the briefing format and must be checked first thing every day.
3. **Warm-referral assets are still time-asymmetric.** A borderline-fresh GitLab posting + a no-warm-up-needed friend referral is worth more than a fresh posting at a cold company. Today's Top Role 1 reflects that — and is the day's only credible application target.
