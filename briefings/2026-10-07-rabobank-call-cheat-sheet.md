**Opening**
> "Thank you, I'm really happy about this."

**Asked for a yes on the phone**
> "I'd like to see the offer in writing, with all the conditions, so I can look at it as a whole. If I have practical questions, I can send them to you, and then I can give you a better answer."
> "Could you send it by email? I'll come back to you by [Mon 12/10]."

**Pushed again, or "take it or leave it"**
> "I'm very positive. I just want to read it properly and check a few practical details first."

**"Any questions now?"**
> "Nothing fundamental. Just practical details, which I'd rather ask once I've read it."

**"Why do you need time?"**
> "I like to review everything on my own time so I can give an informed answer."

**"What are your expectations?"**
> "What did you have in mind within the range?"

If she insists:
> "Between €8,200 and €8,400 gross per month, for the 36-hour week."

**Number below €8,000**
> "That's lower than I expected. I was thinking €8,200 to €8,400. What would it take to get there?"

If she says no:
> "Let me look at the full package in writing."

**Number €8,000–8,200**
> "That's close. Is there room towards €8,300? I'll review it in writing."

**Number €8,200 or higher**
> "That's in the right direction. I'll review it in writing."

**"Current salary?"**
> "I'm freelancing, so nothing compares directly. I'm going by the scale."

**"When can you start?"**
> "I finish a freelance commitment at the end of October, so mid-November. Monday 16th, for example."

If an earlier start comes up, trade it, don't give it:
> "Mid-November is what works for me. If an earlier start matters to the team, I'm open to talking about it, together with the rest of the package."

**"The team is eager to have you start soon"**
> "I'm eager too. That's why I'd like to get the details right, so I can start with full commitment."

**"Secondary benefits"**
> "Which of these are flexible: starting step, extra leave days, training budget, travel and home-working, outside activities?"
