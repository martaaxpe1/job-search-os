If she presses ("can you give me a quick yes so I can start the contract?"):

If she insists on your number:

## What "secondary benefits" (secundaire arbeidsvoorwaarden) means
Everything except base salary. Ask which are flexible; the HM said this is where room exists:
1. **Starting step in scale** and when the first salary review is (in effect this is the base-salary lever)
2. **Sign-on / one-off payment**, less common in NL corporates, ask once, lightly
3. **Extra leave days**, or buying more days with the 10% benefit budget
4. **Training / conference budget** (design conferences, courses)
5. **Travel & home-working**: you live in Rotterdam, role is hybrid in Utrecht. Ask about travel allowance, NS Business Card, home-office allowance, number of office days
6. **Contract**: permanent (vast) from day 1? Probation period length?
7. **Outside activities (nevenwerkzaamheden)**: you disclosed outside interests in the process. Get written confirmation of what is approved (e.g., lign.ai, any freelance)
8. **Start date**
9. Pension and CAO details: usually fixed, but ask for the summary so you can read them
10. Optional, once: whether the role could sit in a higher scale given your Global Head background (the answer may be "no", it costs nothing to ask)

I have not verified what Rabobank actually flexes on. **Ask Youp** this evening: "Do people usually negotiate the starting step, and which of these did you get?"
