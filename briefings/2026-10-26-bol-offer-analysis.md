## 3. Five things in the documents that matter

### 3.1 Your base is fixed for the whole contract, and bol says it priced that in
Starting after 1 October means your first raise is on 1/1/2028, after the contract ends on 30/11/2027. On the call Thijmen said they **"put the salary already a little bit higher"** because you'd skip the first review. He also said they compared you with the Heads of Design.

So the argument I built the €9,500 ask on is already answered, and you said on the call the number was as expected. **My recommendation: drop the base ask.** €9,500 would be worth about €3.2K a year including the bonus effect. Point 3.2 is worth far more, and it's an argument he hasn't heard.

### 3.2 The 2027 bonus needs you employed on 31 December 2027
Handbook 2.5.6: no bonus unless you're employed on 31/12 of that year. The contract ends 30/11/2027. If bol doesn't extend it, you lose the whole 2027 bonus: **~€22–27K, for 11 months' work.**

(Small upside: you'd be employed on 31/12/2026, so you get a 2026 bonus on December's salary, ~€2,300 at target, paid March 2027.)

### 3.3 The non-compete is wide
- 9 months after you leave, for any reason, including bol not renewing.
- The list: Amazon, Alibaba/AliExpress, Zalando, Temu, TikTok, Wehkamp, **Coolblue**, A.S. Watson, ToyChamp, MediaMarkt. Coolblue is a major Rotterdam design employer and a likely freelance client.
- "Employed in any manner by, or involved in, directly or indirectly, with or without compensation" covers freelancing for them too.
- The list is updated every January, so it can grow while you're there.
- Penalty: €500 per breach plus €500 a day.
- bol included the written justification Dutch law requires for a non-compete in a fixed-term contract. It's boilerplate (it says "he or she"), but don't plan on a court striking it. I'm not a lawyer. If you have legal-aid insurance (rechtsbijstand), a quick check of this clause is worth it.

Points 3.2 and 3.3 only bite if bol doesn't renew. Asking for protection in that case costs bol little if they plan to keep you, which is why it's the right ask.

### 3.4 Thijmen offered the car allowance; the lease scheme says pool car
On the call he said you could take the budget as a monthly payout instead of a car. Lease scheme section 3 says that on probation or a fixed-term contract you're assigned a pool car. Section 4.5 gives the **€1,177/month gross allowance** to anyone entitled who doesn't drive a lease car, with commuting reimbursed net and deducted from it.

**Up to €14K a year gross, the biggest open number.** Get his answer in writing.

### 3.5 The title in the letter is "Specialist"
The role you interviewed for is Principal Craft Lead UX, and that's the title you'll want on your CV. Ask for it in the contract.

## 6. What to ask

One ask, three questions, by email. Send tonight or first thing Tuesday: you promised an answer by Wednesday, and point 1 may need HR.

### Draft email

> **Subject:** Offer: a few points before Wednesday
>
> Hi Thijmen,
>
> Thanks again for walking me through the offer today, and for sending everything so quickly. I've read the letter, the handbook and the lease scheme. A few points before I give you my answer:
>
> 1. **Bonus and non-compete, to check I've understood.** The handbook says you need to be employed on 31 December to get that year's bonus, and my contract would end on 30 November 2027. If bol decides not to continue it, does that mean no bonus for 2027, or is it paid pro rata for the months worked? And would the non-compete still apply in that case? If the answer is no bonus, could the contract say it's paid pro rata when bol is the one not continuing?
> 2. **Side work.** I'd like to keep a small freelance practice outside bol hours, with no clients in e-commerce or retail. Is that fine, and is there a process to register or approve it? If the contract has a clause on side activities or IP, could you send me the wording?
> 3. **Lease.** You mentioned I could take the lease budget as a monthly allowance. The lease scheme says fixed-term contracts get a pool car. Can you confirm I can choose the allowance from the start? And if I start with the allowance, could I switch to a pool car later in the year?
> 4. **Title.** Could the contract name the role as Principal Craft Lead UX? The letter says Specialist.
> 5. **Screening.** I've been freelancing since July 2025, so there's no current employer to contact. How does the screening work in that case, and what would you need from me? The offer letter mentions Validata and your email DISA, so I'd also like to know which one it will be.
>
> If point 1 needs a bit more time on your side, that's fine. I'll give you my answer as soon as I have yours.
>
> Best,
> Marta

**If you still want the base ask**, add this as point 1 and renumber. It reuses what you said in September, not the review argument he already answered:
> **Base.** In our first call I mentioned 9,000 to 9,500. Given the scope Ronald described, I'd like to land at €9,500.

### If he pushes back
- **"Bonus rules are company-wide."** → *"Understood. Then the non-compete is the one I'd most like settled."*
- **"The non-compete is standard."** → *"I understand it's standard. My question is only about the case where bol chooses not to continue. Can it lapse then?"* If not: ask to fix the competitor list as it stands at signing.
- **"Which way are you leaning?"** → *"Towards bol, if these points work out."*
- **"What's the other offer?"** → name its strengths (pension, leave), never its base.
