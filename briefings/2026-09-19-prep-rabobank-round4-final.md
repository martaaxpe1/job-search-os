## The "Assertion Instead of Proof Moment" Pattern — The Real Root Cause

This is now confirmed across 5+ instances at 3 different companies (Rabobank R2, Rabobank R3 ×2, bol.com R1, bol.com R2 ×2) and, critically, `interview-history.md` explicitly names it as **a general pattern on any identity/motivation question, not tied to one phrasing.** A final round built around "discussion" is exactly the terrain where this fires — expect it on "what are you looking for," "why should we hire you," "what would you bring that's different," or any variant.

**The fix, stated as a rule to apply live, not just to the specific scripts below:** any answer about who you are or what you want must end with a named, dated example — not a framework, not an assertion. If you catch yourself saying "I bring X" or "I'm someone who Y" without a specific moment attached in the same breath, that's the failure mode happening in real time. Build the habit of ending the sentence with "...for example, at [Company], [specific thing]" as a grammatically inseparable clause — the same fix that's been prescribed for the influence-without-mandate answers at bol.com, now generalized to this whole question family.
