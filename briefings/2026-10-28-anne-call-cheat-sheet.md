| Anne | You |
|---|---|
| "Is there anything we can do?" | *"That's kind of you, thank you. No. I've accepted, and it's about the role."* Decide before the call that the answer is no |
| "Was it the money?" / "Was it the scale?" | *"Mainly the role. It's a level up."* Add only if it's true for you: *"The scale played a part too. Without a path or a date to scale 10, the gap was bigger."* |
| "Did you have this when you accepted?" | *"I was in the final stage with them when I accepted, and I didn't know if it would come through. I accepted because I wanted to join."* Optional, and honest: *"I should have told you I was still in another process."* |
| "What did they offer?" | *"It's a higher level, and the package reflects that."* |
| "We've already let other candidates go" | *"I know, and I'm sorry. That's why I wanted to tell you as early as I could."* |
| "Good luck" / "The door's open" | *"Thank you. I'd like to stay in touch."* |
| She's short or cold | *"I understand. Thank you for taking the call."* Don't fill the silence with more apologies |

**Don't:** give bol's numbers, bring up her email or "trust our system", say "maybe in the future", or apologise more than once.
