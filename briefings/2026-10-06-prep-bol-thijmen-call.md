## 5. Questions he might ask back, and what not to volunteer

**Questions he might ask**
- **"Will you present all of it?"** About eight slides live, the rest read-ahead. Ask him what he'd cut (Q3, Q4).
- **"Can I forward it to Maurice and Matthew?"** Decide before the call. Forwarding means they read it cold, without you. The thesis paragraph helps, and the case was written for them, so I'd say yes. Holding it back so they see it fresh on Thursday is the alternative.
- **"How did you build this, did you use AI?"** Yes, as part of the work, and that is the thesis. Be exact about what was yours (argument, structure, examples) and what the tools did. Don't claim a time spent you can't back up.
- **"Other companies? Salary expectations?"** Honest and general (see section 6). No stages, names or numbers unless you choose to.

**Don't volunteer**
- Your own doubts about the case, such as the density worry. Ask for his honest read and let him form it.
- Gaps already tagged in the deck (hypotheses, the Unspoken summit date). Don't repeat them as apologies.
