# bol vs Rabobank: how to decide

## How to use this

1. **Set your weights today, before Monday.** Once you hear bol's number, money will feel bigger than it is. Give each category in section 4 a weight: 1 (nice to have), 2 (matters) or 3 (could decide it on its own). Keep the 3s to three or four.
2. **Decide your dealbreakers** (section 3). A dealbreaker rules an option out whatever it scores.
3. **Score each option 1–5 per category** once the open facts are in (section 6): Thijmen on Monday, Anne on Monday or Tuesday, bol's written terms by Wednesday.
4. **Multiply and add, then run the gut checks** (section 5). If the total and your gut disagree, change the weights, not the scores. That's where the disagreement is.

## 2. Pros and cons

### Rabobank
**Pros**
- Offer in hand, terms known.
- Best pay per hour worked: 36h with a 21.5% employer pension.
- Family terms well above the legal minimum.
- A manager you know, who backed you.
- Hands-on service design in a long transformation, with room to shape how design works there.
- Easier to do well in year one.

**Cons**
- Scale 09 and the title sit below the posted level and below your seniority. The path to 10 isn't defined yet.
- Fixed-term contract to November 2027.
- A senior IC role: your secondary target. Your own Weakness 3 (overqualified, under-challenged) applies.
- Your career plan's reasons to say no include "pure IC role with no path to team leadership." Whether Rabobank has that path is unknown.
- Financial services isn't one of your industries.
- Side freelancing needs approval and can be refused at 36h.

### bol
**Pros**
- Principal title at Heads-of-Craft level, on the UX Management Team, with a mandate from the CPTO's management team. The closest to your primary target.
- AI and design craft at scale: the topic you've been building toward with lign.ai and your own AI work.
- More cash: any offer in the posted range beats Rabobank on yearly cash, with a bonus on top.
- Up to 8 weeks a year working from abroad: Spain is covered.
- B Corp, with a bonus tied to sustainability goals: close to the sustainability thread in your career.
- A CPTO who's invested in the role and offered training for the stretch.

**Cons**
- A new, very visible role. Year-one success is defined by the CPTO, and you have no authority over the Heads of Design.
- The stretch is real: crisp influence at scale, and your assessment's development areas (Courage, Being Bold).
- More setting direction than doing the design: the posting says you set the direction and others carry it out.
- Pension, hours, family terms and the side-work rule are unknown until Monday or Wednesday.
- In the 2022 cuts about 100 fixed-term contracts weren't renewed. That only matters if bol offers fixed-term.

## 5. Gut checks

- **Same pay.** If both paid exactly the same, which would you take? That's your answer without the money.
- **Two years out.** Which job title, and which story, do you want to tell in your next interview?
- **Worst case.** Picture the realistic worst case of each, a year from now. Which one can you live with?
- **Coin flip.** Give each one a side and flip. Notice what you're hoping for while it's in the air.
