# Leaving Rabobank after signing

**The short version:** signing didn't lock you in. Your Rabobank contract has a valid 1-month probation, so you can end it with immediate effect, with no reason needed and no cost. bol's contract matches the offer and isn't conditional on the screening. One thing to settle before you sign bol: the lease allowance isn't in the contract. Email Thijmen about it, then call Anne. The call doesn't need to wait for his answer.

## 1. What the contracts say

### Rabobank (contract dated 19/10)

"Appointment and term", point 6:

> The appointment is subject to a probationary period of 1 months. During the probationary period, either party may terminate the employment contract with immediate effect.

That covers everything the law asks for: it's in writing, it's the same for both sides, and it's 1 month, the maximum for a one-year contract. The common view in Dutch employment law is that you can use it before your first day.

I checked the copy in your Rabobank folder. If the document you signed in Workday is that same contract, this holds.

**Two clauses that read worse than they are:**

- **1.4, "will not be extended".** This is bol giving the legal notice about non-renewal (art. 7:688) up front, so they never owe you a month's pay for missing the deadline. It's standard wording, not a decision about you. Thijmen told you fixed-term is policy for every hire. Renewal needs a new written agreement either way.
- **1.5, notice period.** If you resign later, you give 2 months' notice by registered post. bol has to give 4.

**The clause that matters: 13.4.** It says the contract supersedes earlier verbal and written agreements, and that includes Thijmen's emails. Most of what he confirmed is in the contract or the handbook anyway (1/12 start, side work, bonus). The lease allowance isn't. The contract only says you're eligible for the lease arrangement, which is the Employment Lease Scheme of 1 October 2025 that came with the offer (you saved it from Gmail on 26/10). Signing means agreeing to it, and two parts of it work against you: section 3 assigns a pool car on probation or a fixed-term contract, and 8.2 lets bol change or end the scheme at any time. The allowance is worth up to €14,124 gross a year, so get it written into the contract itself before you sign. An email from Thijmen isn't enough, because 13.4 supersedes it.

## 8. Why not hold both a bit longer

Legally you could keep Rabobank until closer to 16/11. Don't. Anne is planning around you now, so each day costs her more and makes the call worse. And it buys you almost nothing. bol's contract isn't conditional on screening, and bol's trial period only starts on 1/12, after the Rabobank start date, so holding Rabobank can't protect you against it.
