**Verification rule (per Marta's instruction, May 6, 2026):** if there is any doubt about how recent or active a role posting is, the briefing should flag it explicitly and ask Marta to double-check manually rather than presenting it alongside confirmed-fresh roles. Specifically:

1. Only roles where the search snippet shows a posted date within the last ~14 days, or an explicit "X days ago" / "this week" signal, may be presented as a confirmed Top Role.
2. Roles with no verifiable posting date — even if the URL appears live on a career portal — go in a separate "**Needs manual verification**" section, not in Top Roles. Marta will spot-check and feed back which are real before any resume tailoring is done.
