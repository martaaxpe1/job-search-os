### Known Weaknesses / Watch Areas
- **Domain passion signalling:** At companies with strong cultural identity (consumer brands, sports, lifestyle), specialist panels may not feel the authentic passion fast enough. Adidas final round surfaced this. Mitigation: lead earlier with *why this company specifically* — not just the method.
- **"Inspire the team" presence:** Identity-driven companies want domain vision, not just process excellence. Fix: open with a strong POV on the company's space before demonstrating methodology.
- **Overqualification framing for IC roles:** Hiring managers may flag retention risk. Address proactively: "I'm deliberately choosing to go deep on craft here because..."
- **TMAY pacing under live conditions:** First logged instance of TMAY running significantly over the rehearsed target even with a tight script prepared in advance (Rabobank R1: ~4 min live vs. ~110 sec scripted), and the live version dropped the hard metrics (revenue figures, model counts) that were in the written script. Practice needs to include timed, out-loud rehearsal immediately before the call — not just reading the script silently.
- **"Why this company specifically" tightness — partial improvement:** Rabobank R1 showed the underlying research now exists (sustainability-linked lending, cooperative structure) — a real change from Adidas, where the passion material wasn't there at all. The remaining gap is delivery: it trailed off at the end of the call instead of landing early and confidently. Fix is timing/confidence, not content generation.

### Recommended Practice Areas
- "Why this company specifically?" — must be tighter and more visceral, less generic
- "Tell me about yourself" — practise ending with a company-specific hook, not a general close
- "Where do you see yourself in 3 years?" — land this without implying you want the HM's job
- Time TMAY out loud against a clock before every call — target under 2 minutes even when improvising, and force in at least one hard metric per company mentioned
