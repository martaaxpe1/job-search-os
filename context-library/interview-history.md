- **Overqualification framing for IC roles:** Hiring managers may flag retention risk. Address proactively: "I'm deliberately choosing to go deep on craft here because..."

Next drill: restructure the TMAY so the metric is the literal first sentence (can't be dropped if it's not saved for later), and rehearse it immediately after inducing mild pressure (e.g., timed, cold, no re-reads) rather than in a calm mock setting.
- **"Why this company specifically" — still unlanded, now 2 real rounds running.** The EIB/sustainability-linked-lending script has existed since before Round 1 and has not been delivered live yet in either real round (R1: trailed off vagely; R2: never came up at all — technical issues and portfolio depth ate the time budget). Pattern: prepared unprompted material keeps getting crowded out by reactive Q&A. Fix: volunteer it proactively in the first 10 minutes next time, don't wait for a natural opening that isn't coming.
- **Abstract answers on "why does this matter / what would you uniquely add" questions.** Rabobank R2 — both "if you weren't there, what would've happened?" and the direct response to the interviewer's stated ambiguity/support-system concern were answered with assertions ("I brought that product perspective") rather than a specific proof moment. ### Recommended Practice Areas
- "Why this company specifically?" — must be tighter and more visceral, less generic, AND must actually get said out loud in the room (2/2 real rounds so far it hasn't landed)
- "Tell me about yourself" — practise ending with a company-specific hook, not a general close; put the hard metric in the first sentence, not buried mid-story
- "Where do you see yourself in 3 years?" — land this without implying you want the HM's job
- Time TMAY out loud against a clock before every call — target under 2 minutes even when improvising, and force in at least one hard metric per company mentioned
- **New: "How do you operate with less structure/support than your recent projects?"** — build a tight, specific answer anchored in Story 5 (Culligan innovation pipeline, built from zero) and Story 7 (lign.ai, solo freelance, no team) before the next Rabobank round
- **New: retrieval drills, not just content drills.** The gap isn't missing material — it's surfacing the right story in the moment. Practice mock questions that require picking the right story from several options under time pressure, not just delivering one pre-selected story well.
